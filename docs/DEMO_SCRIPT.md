# Demo script — 3 minutes

The recorded walkthrough is `artifacts/demo.mp4` (59 seconds, silent, with
on-screen captions). Use this script for a narrated version.

Record at 1080p. Have the app already running (`make api`) at
`http://127.0.0.1:8000`. Keep the terminal visible in scene 3.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

> "Petrol and diesel prices are the most politically sensitive numbers in
> Pakistan. Every fortnight the price changes, and everyone has a theory. We
> built a system that answers price questions with a citation on every number —
> and refuses when the data does not support an answer."

Show the **Overview** tab.

## 0:20–0:50 — The data is real and governed

Point at the black price board:

- "Petrol Super: 396.06 PKR per litre, week ended 15 October 2026, from the
  Pakistan Bureau of Statistics."
- "High-speed diesel: 398.89 PKR per litre, same week and source. The change
  against the prior week is printed under each price."

Open `data/manifest.csv` in the editor:

> "Every raw file — 41 PBS weekly reports, nine PBS city annexes, 11 EIA daily
> series, the World Bank Pink Sheet — is SHA-256 hashed with its URL, publisher,
> fetch time and licence. 91,672 clean rows. No synthetic data, no Kaggle
> mirrors."

## 0:50–1:20 — The model beats its declared baseline

Switch to the **Model** tab. Point at the interval band and the capped 2020
spike:

> "We declared persistence as the baseline. On next-month Brent volatility, our
> HAR ridge model cuts MAE by 7.3% — 17.80 to 16.51 vol points — on a
> chronological 80/20 split. The 90% interval actually covers 89.3% of held-out
> months. Errors by year and volatility regime are published, including the bad
> ones."

Point at the price-level note:

> "We also tried forecasting the price level. It did not meaningfully beat the
> random walk, and we say so instead of hiding it."

## 1:20–2:20 — The agent answers — and refuses

Switch to **Insight Agent**.

Ask:

1. `What is the current petrol price in Pakistan?`
   - Answer: 396.06 PKR/L, 2026-10-15, `[PBS-SPI]`, row id shown.
2. `What was the diesel price in Karachi in September 2026?`
   - Answer: 401.14 PKR/L, city fact, row id shown.
3. `What is the weather in Lahore today?`
   - **Refusal.** "This is outside the declared scope."

Point at the **Evidence** panel on the right:

> "Every answer returns the citation tag, the source row ids and the retrieval
> score. The number is quoted from the gold table, not generated by a language
> model."

Ask in Urdu: `پاکستان میں پیٹرول کی قیمت کیا ہے؟`
- Answer in Urdu, same number and citation.

## 2:20–2:50 — Automation and reproducibility

Terminal:

```bash
make all
```

> "One command re-downloads and re-hashes every source, runs the quality agent,
> rebuilds the gold layer, refits the model, rebuilds the vector index, runs the
> tests, regenerates the summary PDF and exports the Prefect run history."

Show `prefect_runs.csv`:

> "These are real runs with real timestamps. We missed the requested Tue–Wed
> window, and the log says so; we did not backfill it."

## 2:50–3:00 — Close

> "Fuel prices, with receipts. Every number traceable, every refusal
> deliberate."
